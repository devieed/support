This privacy policy describes the Z-Rex support website at support.z-rex.net. It does not replace the privacy policy of any individual app or game. Those documents live on their own pages, linked from each product’s listing.

Who we are

The site is published by Z-Rex. For privacy questions about this website, write to support@z-rex.net.

What this website is for

The site lists Z-Rex apps and games, links to download pages, links to each product’s own privacy policy and terms, and explains how to join testing. It does not sell products directly and it does not require an account.

Information this site handles

  • Language preference. If you choose a language, or if the site detects one on your first visit, that choice is stored in your browser’s local storage under the key zx-lang. It stays on your device. We do not receive it on a server.
  • Messages you send. If you email us, or if you use a mailto link, we receive whatever you include in that message, such as your address and the text. We use it to answer you and to manage test access.
  • Hosting logs. The site is static. GitHub and Cloudflare, which may host or proxy it, can process technical data such as IP address, user agent, and the URL requested, under their own terms, for security and operations.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies and does not run its own analytics. Your browser may still store the language preference described above. If a host later adds a strictly necessary security cookie, it is covered by that host’s policy.

Other sites

Download buttons, test-group links, and policy links leave this website. Google Play, the App Store, and each app’s own policy pages have their own rules. Follow those pages for how a specific app handles data.

Testing

Joining a test group happens through email or through an external link you configure, such as a Google Play testing page or a group you already use. Those services process your participation under their own policies. Test builds are unfinished and may collect different data than the released app. Read the in-test disclosure and the product’s privacy policy before you join.

Children

This support site is a general catalog. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them through this site.

Retention

The language preference remains until you clear site data for this domain or change it. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request and our ordinary records, then deleted or archived.

Your choices

You can change the language in the header at any time, and you can clear local storage in your browser. You can ask us what emails we still have from you, or ask us to delete them, by writing to the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
